Products & stock
Availability can change. Send the product name or screenshot to the seller and ask them to confirm current stock before paying.
The seller’s social posts say orders may start from one piece and that designs can be mixed. Confirm the minimum quantity and options for your chosen items before placing an order.
Ask for the currently available colour and size list for each design. Not every option may be in stock at the same time.
Colours and details can look different on screens. Request recent, unedited photos or a short video of the exact item, plus fabric and finishing details, before paying.
Pricing & payment
The prices shown are indicative entries based on the supplied ad text and must be matched to the correct products by the seller. Confirm the final price, quantity, delivery fee and any other charges in writing.
The social posts appear to say full payment is required upfront. Verify the payment policy directly with the seller before transferring money, and request written confirmation of your order.
Ask the seller whether bulk rates or discounts are available for your quantity and selected designs. Get the offer and total amount confirmed in writing.
Delivery & support
The supplied social posts mention free delivery and a 24-hour timeframe, but coverage and timing have not been independently verified. Confirm whether delivery is free to your location and ask for a realistic dispatch and arrival estimate.
Before paying, ask how the seller confirms orders and whether a courier receipt or tracking number will be provided. Save all messages and receipts.
Ask the seller for their written exchange, return, damaged-item and cancellation policy before paying. Do not assume returns or refunds are available unless the seller confirms them.
Trust & business
Review independent customer feedback, ask for recent order proof with private details hidden, verify the business contact details, and consider a small test order. Social media claims alone do not guarantee a seller’s legitimacy.
You can contact the seller for repeat orders. Restock availability, wholesale rates and quality consistency should be confirmed for each order.
